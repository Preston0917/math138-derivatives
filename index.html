Memory tricks
  • “Co” = minus. Every co-function (cos, cot, csc) has a minus sign in its derivative.
  • Pairs: tan ↔ sec², cot ↔ −csc².
  • sec → sec·tan, csc → −csc·cot. The function keeps itself and adds its partner.
  • sin → cos → −sin → −cos → sin (the cycle repeats every 4 derivatives).
